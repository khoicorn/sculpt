Rules

Your week

  • 4 lifts: Glutes A, Push, Glutes B, Pull.
  • Plus 1-3 classes: Pump, Combat, Yoga.
  • Each workout: do every exercise, top to bottom.
  • Glutes A and B: 2+ days apart.

Heart

  • No HIIT. Cardio = warm-up only.
  • Stop every set with 2-3 reps left.
  • Rest timer locks the next set. Skip only after 90 s.
  • Resting HR over 100 = Yoga or walk.
  • Class: HR over 150 = walk until under 120.
  • Combat max 1x per week.

Pelvic floor

  • No barbell squat. No heavy deadlift.
  • Leg press: light, feet high, stop at 90°.
  • Exhale on every push. Never hold breath.
  • Rectal pressure = stop, walk 2 min, swap.

Weights

  • App suggests today's kg for you.
  • After each set tap Easy, Good or Hard.
  • All reps done = app adds weight next time.
